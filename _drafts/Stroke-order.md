Letters in rook script have a prescribed stroke order. Similar to writing in east Asian languages, the stroke order is as important as the exact shapes of letters, and when writing quickly the exact shapes of the letters may vary greatly so stroke order should be used to figure out what letters were written.

At a high level, the stroke order of consonants is left to right, vowels go left to right, and both go top to bottom.
