      I considered other updates to the official form of rook script syllables when adding descenders to some vowels. In the first image, vowels are written left-to-right like consonants, but the overall structure of syllables is otherwise unchanged. The second aims to reduce the amount of back-and-forth wrist movement when writing vowels left-to-right by dropping the onset to align vertically with vowels. To differentiate vowel forms from consonant forms, all vowels are written with an onset, using the letter ng as a blank onset (similar to Hangul).
