      Originally rook script had very distinct regular and cursive styles, with regular written horizontally and cursive written vertically. The cursive style had a number of rules for combining some strokes and lifting the pen between others, and some of the letter forms were influenced by the cursive forms. I still write in multiple styles, but there are fewer firm distinctions between them, and all styles can be written horizontally or vertically
